Understand Air Pollution

How can we reduce air pollution?

  • Use public transport or share rides when practical.
  • Avoid burning leaves and household waste.
  • Walk or cycle when it is safe to do so.
  • Plant and care for suitable local trees.
WATER QUALITY

Protect Every Drop

Plastic waste, sewage and harmful chemicals can contaminate water bodies.

💧

Save Water

Close taps properly, fix leaks and avoid wasting water.

🚯

Prevent Water Pollution

Never throw plastic, oil or chemicals into rivers and drains.

🧪

Check Water Quality

Use suitable testing methods and trusted sources to assess water safety.

🌳

Plant More Trees

Plant suitable local species and care for existing trees to support greener communities.

🚰

Save Water

Fix leaks, turn taps off when not needed, and use water thoughtfully at home and school.

♻️

Reduce Plastic

Choose reusable items where possible and sort waste for proper collection and recycling.

🚌

Use Public Transport

Use buses, trains, shared rides, walking, or cycling when practical and safe.

🌊

Keep Water Sources Clean

Keep litter, oils, and chemicals out of drains, streams, ponds, and rivers.

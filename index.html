AIR QUALITY

Understand Air Pollution

Vehicle exhaust, industrial emissions, waste burning and dust can pollute the air.

Example values only — these are not actual measurements or percentages.

How can we reduce air pollution?

  • Use public transport or share rides when practical.
  • Avoid burning leaves and household waste.
  • Walk or cycle when it is safe to do so.
  • Plant and care for suitable local trees.
WATER QUALITY

Protect Every Drop

Plastic waste, sewage and harmful chemicals can contaminate water bodies.

💧

Save Water

Close taps properly, fix leaks and avoid wasting water.

🚯

Prevent Water Pollution

Never throw plastic, oil or chemicals into rivers and drains.

🧪

Check Water Quality

Use suitable testing methods and trusted sources to assess water safety.

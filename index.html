About the project

A healthier environment starts with awareness.

Clean air and safe water support human health, protect wildlife, and help communities thrive. This college awareness project explains common pollution sources and encourages everyday action.

↗

01 / Breathe

Clean air

Healthy air matters for breathing, wellbeing, plants, animals, and the quality of life in our communities.

⌁

02 / Protect

Clean water

Safe water is essential for drinking, cooking, farming, sanitation, and the survival of aquatic life.

✳

03 / Sustain

A shared responsibility

Learning about pollution helps people make informed choices and support a cleaner, more sustainable future.

Air quality

What makes the air polluted?

Air pollution happens when harmful gases, smoke, dust, or tiny particles enter the air. It can affect people, animals, plants, and the wider environment.

🚗

Common source

Vehicle emissions

Cars, buses, and other vehicles can release gases and particles. Walking, cycling, carpooling, and public transport can help reduce emissions.

🏭

Common source

Industrial smoke

Some industrial processes release pollutants. Suitable pollution controls, cleaner energy, and monitoring can help limit emissions.

♨

Common source

Burning waste

Burning plastic and mixed waste can release harmful pollutants. Sorting waste and using safe collection or recycling services are better choices.

i

Remember: This website provides educational information. It does not measure or report live Air Quality Index (AQI) readings.

Water quality

Keep our water sources clean.

Water pollution occurs when waste, sewage, or harmful substances contaminate rivers, lakes, groundwater, or other water sources.

Every drop matters
01

Plastic waste

Plastic litter can reach drains, rivers, lakes, and oceans. Reusable items and responsible disposal help reduce this pollution.

02

Untreated sewage

Untreated sewage can introduce germs and pollutants into water, creating risks for people and aquatic life.

03

Chemical pollution

Some chemicals from homes, farms, and industries can contaminate water. Proper handling and disposal are important.

i

Remember: Clear-looking water is not necessarily safe to drink. Use trusted local health and water authorities for safety guidance. This website does not report live water-quality measurements.

Why it matters

Pollution affects more than the environment.

Reducing pollution can help protect health, natural habitats, and the resources that communities depend on.

☼

Human health

Air pollution can affect breathing and overall health. Contaminated water can also carry health risks.

🌡

Climate and nature

Some air pollutants contribute to warming and environmental changes that put pressure on ecosystems.

🐟

Aquatic life

Waste and harmful substances in water can damage habitats and threaten fish and other aquatic organisms.

🌱

Natural habitats

Pollution can damage soil, plants, and habitats that support biodiversity.

Take action

Simple habits make a difference.

Protecting the environment is a shared responsibility. Start with achievable choices at home, school, college, and in the community.

✿

Plant and protect trees

Trees support ecosystems. Choose suitable local species and care for them as they grow.

↻

Reduce plastic and waste

Carry reusable bags and bottles, separate waste, and use local recycling options where available.

滴

Use water carefully

Fix leaks, avoid unnecessary waste, and keep oils, chemicals, and litter out of drains and waterways.

↗

Choose cleaner travel

Walk, cycle, share rides, or use public transport when practical and safe.

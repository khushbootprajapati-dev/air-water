ABOUT OUR PROJECT

Why Air & Water Quality Matters?

Clean air and safe water are essential for human health, environmental balance and sustainable development.

This project focuses on creating awareness about air and water pollution and understanding people's awareness through a survey.

AIR QUALITY

Understanding Air Pollution

🌫️

Vehicle Pollution

Vehicle emissions release harmful gases and particles into the atmosphere.

🏭

Industrial Smoke

Industrial activities may release smoke and harmful pollutants into the air.

🔥

Burning Waste

Burning plastic and other waste can release dangerous pollutants.

WATER QUALITY

Protecting Our Water Resources

🧴

Plastic Waste

Plastic waste can enter rivers, lakes and other water sources.

🚰

Untreated Sewage

Untreated sewage can contaminate water and harm human and aquatic life.

☣️

Chemical Pollution

Harmful chemicals can contaminate water resources and affect ecosystems.

TAKE ACTION

What Can We Do?

🌳

Plant More Trees

Trees help improve air quality and support a healthier environment.

♻️

Reduce Plastic

Reduce single-use plastic and dispose of waste responsibly.

💧

Save Water

Avoid unnecessary water wastage and protect valuable water resources.

🚌

Use Public Transport

Public transport can help reduce vehicle emissions and air pollution.
